LOYOLA UNIVERSITY MARYLAND - SELLINGER SCHOOL OF BUSINESS
CAREER PATHWAYS BY MAJOR
======================================================================

ACCOUNTING, BBA
───────────────
Entry-Level Roles: Staff Accountant, Audit Associate, Tax Associate
Mid-Career: Senior Accountant, Audit Manager, Tax Manager, Controller
Senior: CFO, Partner (Big 4/regional firm), VP Finance
Certifications: CPA (requires 150 credits), CMA, CIA
Top Employers: Big 4 (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC), regional firms, corporations
Grad School: Master of Accounting (Loyola 3+1 or 4+1), MBA

BUSINESS ECONOMICS, BBA
───────────────────────
Entry-Level: Economic Analyst, Research Associate, Financial Analyst
Mid-Career: Senior Economist, Policy Analyst, Business Strategist
Senior: Chief Economist, VP Strategy, Director of Research
Certifications: CFA (with finance courses), data analytics certs
Top Employers: Federal Reserve, consulting firms, think tanks, banks
Grad School: MA/PhD Economics, MBA, Public Policy

FINANCIAL RISK MANAGEMENT & INSURANCE, BS
──────────────────────────────────────────
Entry-Level: Underwriting Analyst, Risk Analyst, Claims Analyst, Actuarial Analyst
Mid-Career: Risk Manager, Senior Underwriter, Actuarial Associate
Senior: Chief Risk Officer, VP Risk, Partner (insurance/consulting)
Certifications: CPCU, ARM, FRM, actuarial exams (SOA/CAS)
Top Employers: Insurance companies, reinsurers, banks, consulting firms
Grad School: MS Finance, MS Actuarial Science, MBA

FORENSIC ACCOUNTING, BBA

Top Employers: FBI, SEC, Big 4 forensic practices, law firms, insurance
Grad School: Master of Accounting, MS Criminal Justice, JD

FINANCE, BS
───────────
Entry-Level: Financial Analyst, Investment Banking Analyst, Corporate Finance Analyst, Credit Analyst, Wealth Management Associate
Mid-Career: Portfolio Manager, Equity Research Associate, FP&A Manager, Treasury Manager, Commercial Lender
Senior: CFO, Managing Director, Chief Investment Officer, Treasurer
Certifications: CFA, CAIA, FRM, CFP, Series 7/63/65 (employer-sponsored)
Elective fit: Investments track → FI 431, FI 433, FI 434, FI 380; Corporate/IB track → FI 340, FI 330 (sophomores), AC 301/302; Risk track → FRI 300/305/410; Real estate → FI/REE 225, 325, REE 445; Data-heavy roles → IS 420, IS 460, EC 420
Experiential: FI 434 Student Managed Sellinger Applied Portfolio Fund; FI 499 internship; CFA Institute Research Challenge team
Top Employers: Banks, asset managers, corporate finance departments, Big 4 advisory, insurers
Grad School: MS Finance, MBA, CFA charter path

SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT, BBA
────────────────────────────
Entry-Level: Supply Chain Analyst, Procurement/Sourcing Analyst, Logistics Coordinator, Operations Analyst
Mid-Career: Supply Chain Manager, Category Manager, Logistics Manager, Demand Planner
Senior: VP Supply Chain, Chief Procurement Officer, COO
Certifications: APICS/ASCM CSCP or CPIM, Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, PMP (with OM 335)
Track fit: IS & Data Analytics track for analytics roles; Quantitative track for planning/forecasting; International Trade track for global sourcing; Sustainability track for ESG supply chain roles
Grad School: MS Supply Chain Management, MBA
