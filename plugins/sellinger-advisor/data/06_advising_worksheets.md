## PROGRAM NOTES PAGE (FRMI / Real Estate worksheets) — "Important Notes"
1. GATEWAY COURSES — take in the FIRST YEAR (internship-market readiness): EC 102, AC 201 or BH 200, EC 103, FI 230 or BH 230, EC 220 or ST 210.
2. STUDY ABROAD — talk to International Programs, AASC and the advisor in freshman or sophomore year. FRI-coded major courses CANNOT be fulfilled abroad; the department can't guarantee sequence/availability for students who go abroad. Traditionally easiest: SPRING of junior year. While abroad, take Liberal Arts Core, Business Core, or free electives.
3. DOUBLE MAJORING — consult advisors in both majors early; it usually uses many free-elective slots.
