# Sellinger Program Requirements — Isda Supplychain — 2026–2027 Undergraduate Catalogue
Source: catalogue.loyola.edu (catoid=43), program pages pulled 2026-10-06. poid = catalog program ID (URL: https://catalogue.loyola.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=43&poid=XXXX).
AUTHORITY: authoritative for 2026–27 program requirements. A student's DegreeWorks audit governs THEIR catalog year — if the audit's catalog year is earlier and differs from this file, follow the audit and flag the difference.
Every BBA and BS (Finance, FRMI, Real Estate) major ALSO completes the six-course Business Foundation (AC 201, FI 230, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260), EC 102, EC 103, MA 151 or MA 251, EC 220 (or ST 210, except Business Economics), the Loyola Core, and 2 Diversity-Justice courses — see 03_policies_core_and_rules.md.
Sample plans ("typical program") are ONE possible path, not requirements.

## Information Systems, Data Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence for Business, BBA (poid 5916)  ("ISDA/ISDAAI")
Major requirements: IS 352, EC 102, EC 103, EC 220 or ST 210, IS 353, IS 355, IS 358, IS 420, IS 458, LW 305, MA 151 or MA 251, an Elective, and IS 453 OR IS 454.
Courses that may be taken as free electives: IS 301, IS 310, IS 311, IS 330, IS 356, IS 360, IS 365, IS 366, IS 367, IS 368, IS 452, IS 453, IS 454, IS 459, IS 460, IS 470, IS 499, LW 414, OM 305, OM 334, OM 335, OM 406. Optional two-course Cybersecurity micro-credential within free electives.
Notes: With department chair approval CS 151 may substitute for IS 352. Room for a double major or minor.
Typical plan: Y1 F: EC 102, EN 101, HS 100, MA 151/251, WR 100 | Y1 S: EC 103, EC 220/ST 210, IS 251, Nat Sci, Language | Y2 F: AC 201, IS 353, MK 240, PL 201, EN/HS 200-level | Y2 S: IS 358, IS 420, OM 260, TH 201, Elective | Y3 F: IS 352, IS 453 or IS 454, MG 201, Fine Arts, Elective | Y3 S: FI 230, IS 355, PL/TH 200-level, 2 Electives | Y4 F: LW 305, 4 Electives | Y4 S: IS 458 (Spring only), Ethics, 3 Electives.
Sequencing: IS 458 requires IS 352 (or CS 151), IS 353, IS 358 and is Spring only. IS 420 is Fall only in the 2026–27 catalog, yet the sample plan places it in sophomore spring — flag for advisor if planning IS 420 in a spring term.

Required: AC 202, EC 102, EC 103, EC 220, LW 305, MA 151 or MA 251, MG 402, OM 305, OM 334, OM 335, OM 406.

- Information Systems & Data Analytics: DS 303, IS 301, IS 353, IS 358, IS 360, IS 420, IS 452, IS 453, IS 458, IS 460, MK 415
- International Trade & Relationship Management: EC 304, EC 440, EC 446, FI 340, IB 315, IB 372, IB 482, LW 410
- Quantitative & Statistical Methods: EC 405, EC 420, EC 425, MA 481, MA 485, MK 441, ST 310, ST 471
- Sustainability & Environmental Management: EC 360, LW 411, MG 222, MG 433
Sequencing (2026–27 catalog): OM 305 Fall only; OM 334 SPRING only; OM 335 Fall only; OM 406 Fall/Spring. [Sample plan not extracted; see catalog.]
