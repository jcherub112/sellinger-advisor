# Sellinger Program Requirements — Accounting Economics — 2026–2027 Undergraduate Catalogue
Source: catalogue.loyola.edu (catoid=43), program pages pulled 2026-10-06. poid = catalog program ID (URL: https://catalogue.loyola.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=43&poid=XXXX).
AUTHORITY: authoritative for 2026–27 program requirements. A student's DegreeWorks audit governs THEIR catalog year — if the audit's catalog year is earlier and differs from this file, follow the audit and flag the difference.
Every BBA and BS (Finance, FRMI, Real Estate) major ALSO completes the six-course Business Foundation (AC 201, FI 230, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260), EC 102, EC 103, MA 151 or MA 251, EC 220 (or ST 210, except Business Economics), the Loyola Core, and 2 Diversity-Justice courses — see 03_policies_core_and_rules.md.
Sample plans ("typical program") are ONE possible path, not requirements.

## Accounting, BBA (poid 5913)
Major requirements: AC 202, AC 301, AC 302, AC 303, AC 311, AC 401, AC 402, AC 412, AC 421, EC 102, EC 103, LW 406, and MG 306 OR MG 402.
Notes: Six of the eight upper-level AC courses usually must be taken at Loyola. Scholars take LW 406 in place of BH 305. ST 210 may substitute for EC 220.
CPA: 150 credit hours needed to sit for the CPA (varies by state; nasba.org). Loyola MAcc (10 courses / 30 credits); accounting students may apply for early admission after junior year and take up to two MAcc courses senior year.
Typical plan: Y1 F: EC 102, MA 151/251, PL 201, WR 100, Language | Y1 S: EC 103, EN 101, HS 100, IS 251, Fine Arts | Y2 F: AC 201, EC 220, MG 201, TH 201, Elective | Y2 S: AC 202, FI 230, MK 240, PL/TH 200-level, EN/HS 200-level | Y3 F: AC 301, AC 311, OM 260, Nat Sci, Elective | Y3 S: AC 302, AC 402, 3 Electives | Y4 F: AC 303, AC 412, LW 406, Ethics, Elective | Y4 S: AC 401, AC 421, MG 306 or MG 402, 2 Electives.

## Forensic Accounting, BBA (poid 6206)
Required: AC 202, AC 301, one of AC 302/AC 303/AC 401, AC 311, AC 411, AC 412, AC 421, EC 102, EC 103, EC 220 (ST 210 may substitute), FO 100 or FO 101, LW 406, MA 151 or MA 251.
THREE forensic accounting electives from: FI 322, FI 440, SC 100, SC 207, SC 260, SC 435.
Note: AC 411 is restricted to forensic accounting majors (Spring only).

## Business Economics, BBA (poid 5914) — also Quantitative Specialization (poid 5976)
Major requirements: AC 202, EC 102, EC 103, EC 301, EC 302, LW 305, MG 402, plus FOUR 300/400-level EC courses (at least TWO at the 400 level). Department chair may approve certain outside courses toward the upper-level requirement.
Notes: Ideally finish EC 301 or EC 302 before fall of junior year. Strong math students: MA 251 + MA 252; grad-school bound: MA 301. ST 210 does NOT substitute for EC 220 in Business Economics. Accelerated BS→MS Data Science: ST 710/ST 765 may count as EC electives. ELMBA accelerated: EL 707/EL 715 in lieu of EC 341.
Typical plan: Y1 F: EC 102, MA 151/251, WR 100, Language, Elective | Y1 S: EC 103, EC 220 or Nat Sci, HS 100, Fine Arts, Language/Elective | Y2 F: AC 201, EC 220 or Nat Sci, EN 101, MK 240, PL 201 or TH 201 | Y2 S: AC 202, EC 301 or EC 302, IS 251, PL 201 or TH 201, EN/HS 200-level | Y3 F: EC 301 or EC 302, LW 305, MG 201, PL/TH 200-level, Elective | Y3 S: FI 230, OM 260, 3 Electives | Y4 F: MG 402 or Elective, 2 EC electives, 2 Electives | Y4 S: MG 402 or Elective, PL 310, 2 EC electives, Elective.
