Source: catalogue.loyola.edu (catoid=43), program pages pulled 2026-10-06. poid = catalog program ID (URL: https://catalogue.loyola.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=43&poid=XXXX).
AUTHORITY: authoritative for 2026–27 program requirements. A student's DegreeWorks audit governs THEIR catalog year — if the audit's catalog year is earlier and differs from this file, follow the audit and flag the difference.
Every BBA and BS (Finance, FRMI, Real Estate) major ALSO completes the six-course Business Foundation (AC 201, FI 230, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260), EC 102, EC 103, MA 151 or MA 251, EC 220 (or ST 210, except Business Economics), the Loyola Core, and 2 Diversity-Justice courses — see 03_policies_core_and_rules.md.

## Sellinger Scholars Program (poid 5912) — honors overlay, not a major
Open to Sellinger majors: Accounting, Business Economics, Finance BS, FRMI BS, ISDA(AI), International Business, Management, Marketing, Real Estate BS, Sustainability Management.
Required (BH replaces regular foundation course in parentheses): BH 196 Internship Prep (first year); BH 199 Senior Seminar; BH 200 (AC 201); BH 201 (MG 201); BH 230 (FI 230); BH 240 (MK 240); BH 251 (IS 251); BH 260 (OM 260); BH 305 (LW 305) — Accounting majors take BH/LW 406 Commercial Law instead; BH 403 Scholars Consulting Practicum.
Standards each semester: full-time (12+ credits; less needs program director approval); minimum 3.300 cumulative GPA; at least one internship (two recommended); full participation in cohort courses, BH 196 and BH 199; annual community service hours. Falling short = loss of eligibility (written appeal to academic director within 10 business days after grades post). Scholars may request permission to take ONE BH cohort course outside the cohort (at Loyola or abroad).

## International Business, BBA (poid 5917)
Required: EC 102, EC 103, EC 304 or EC 348, IB 282, IB 315 or MG 315, IB 372, LW 305, MG 402, MG 407.
First required elective (one of): EC 304, EC 348, EC 430, EC 440, EC 446, FI 340, IB 370, IB 482, IB 499, LW 410, MG 333, OM 334.
Second required elective (one of): EC 304, EC 348, EC 430, EC 440, EC 446, FI 315, FI 340, HS 221, HS 226, HS 227, HS 245, HS 246, HS 247, IB 300, IB 370, IB 482, IB 499, IS 360, LW 410, MG 333, MG 451, ML 307, ML 310, ML 315, ML 392, OM 334, PS 302, PS 308, PS 309, PS 336, PS 355, PS 357, PS 365, SN 303, SN 304.
Global Experiential Requirement (one; international students exempt): IB 371 or MG 371; IB 300; IB 499; non-credit IB-related internship (domestic or international); a study abroad course beyond the 104 level; short-term study abroad program; a Loyola language course beyond 104; an IB-related service-learning course; an IB-related Loyola immersion; WTCI Albrecht Fellowship; or an alternative approved by the Management & Organizations chair. Complete junior or senior year.
Double counting: other than shared Business Foundation and shared required courses, only ONE course may double count between International Business and Sustainability Management majors/minors.
Typical plan: Y1 F: EC 102, EN 101, MA 151/251, WR 100, Language | Y1 S: EC 103, HS 100, Fine Arts, Nat Sci, Elective | Y2 F: AC 201, EC 220, MK 240, PL 201, EN/HS 200-level | Y2 S: IB 282 (by sophomore year), IS 251, MG 201, TH 201, Elective | Y3 F: EC 304 or EC 348, FI 230, IB 315/MG 315, PL/TH 200-level, Elective | Y3 S: LW 305, IB 499, OM 260, IB Elective, Elective | Y4 F: IB 372, MG 402, 3 Electives | Y4 S: MG 407, Ethics, 3 Electives.

DATA NOTE: MG 433 carries a "Restricted to seniors" restriction in the course listing, but the sample plan places it in junior spring. Flag for advisor.

## Interdisciplinary Major in Management/Psychology (poid 5971) — BA/BS, NOT a BBA
Required: MG 201, MG 304, MG 305, MG 306, MG 402, MG 407, PY 101, PY 200, PY 291, PY 292, one PY 400-level mastery course, ST 110 or ST 210 or EC 220; two management electives (MG 315 or MG 415, MG 333, MG 410, MG 412, MG 414, MG 419, MG 420, MG 453, MG 499); four PY electives at the 200 level or higher (e.g., PY 201, 203, 221, 222, 261, 262, 325, 351). Declare by sophomore year. Can only be combined with majors outside Sellinger; does not earn a BBA.
