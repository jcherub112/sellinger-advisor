# Sellinger Program Requirements — Minors And Closed Programs — 2026–2027 Undergraduate Catalogue
Source: catalogue.loyola.edu (catoid=43), program pages pulled 2026-10-06. poid = catalog program ID (URL: https://catalogue.loyola.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=43&poid=XXXX).
AUTHORITY: authoritative for 2026–27 program requirements. A student's DegreeWorks audit governs THEIR catalog year — if the audit's catalog year is earlier and differs from this file, follow the audit and flag the difference.
Every BBA and BS (Finance, FRMI, Real Estate) major ALSO completes the six-course Business Foundation (AC 201, FI 230, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260), EC 102, EC 103, MA 151 or MA 251, EC 220 (or ST 210, except Business Economics), the Loyola Core, and 2 Diversity-Justice courses — see 03_policies_core_and_rules.md.
Sample plans ("typical program") are ONE possible path, not requirements.

## MINORS
### Accounting Minor (poid 5968)
AC 201, AC 202, AC 301, AC 409 or AC 412, plus two of AC 302, AC 303, AC 311, AC 401, AC 402, AC 421. Suggested "Entrepreneur track": AC 409, AC 311, AC 402. "CPA track": AC 412, AC 302 or AC 303, AC 421.
### Business Minor (poid 5920) — NOT available to any Sellinger major
AC 201, EC 102, plus any four of AC 202, FI 230, IB 282, IS 251, LW 305, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260. Loyola MBA hopefuls: add EC 103.
### Business Economics Minor (poid 5921) — not available to Economics BA majors
EC 102, EC 103, EC 301 or EC 302, plus four more EC courses (one at the 400 level). EC 220 counts only if EC 420 is completed.
### Finance Minor (poid 5973)
FI 230, FI 322, plus four of FI 330, FI 340, FI 350, FI 380, FI 426, FI 430, FI 431, FI 433, FI 434, FI 440, FI 441, FI 499. AC 201 and EC 102 before FI 230; EC 220/ST 210 before or with FI 230. FI 330 is sophomore-only and needs AC 201 + FI 230 first.
### Information Systems and Data Analytics Minor (poid 5923) — not for ISDA majors
15 credits: EC 220 or ST 210; IS 251; IS 353; IS 358 or IS 420 (Data Analytics track) or IS 453 (Information Systems track); plus one of AC 402, CS 151, IS 301, IS 310, IS 311, IS 352, IS 355, IS 356, IS 358, IS 360, IS 365, IS 366, IS 367, IS 368, IS 420, IS 452, IS 453, IS 454, IS 459, IS 460, IS 470, IS 499, LW 414, MK 415, OM 305, OM 334, OM 335, OM 406.
### International Business Minor (poid 5924) — not for IB majors
IB 282, IB 315 or MG 315, IB 372, MG 201; first elective: one of EC 304, EC 348, EC 430, EC 440, EC 446, FI 340, IB 370, IS 360, IB 482, LW 410, MG 333, OM 334; second elective: one from the IB major's second-elective list (see International Business, BBA).
### Marketing Minor (poid 5925) — not for Marketing majors
MK 240, MK 346, MK 440, MK 441 or MK 442, plus two of MK 349, MK 401, MK 415, MK 444, MK 447, MK 448, MK 449, MK 450, MK 451, MK 452, MK 453, MK 499.
### Innovation and Entrepreneurship Minor (poid 5937)
18 credits: BA 205, BA 401, BA 490, MG 451, plus two electives from a broad list incl. BA 350, CM 304, CM 308, CM 312, CM 321, CM 365, CM 375, CS 403/DS 303, CS 456, EC 304, EC 347, EC 405, EC 446, EC 450, EG 103, EG 227/SA 227, FI 230, FI 322, FI 350, IB 315, IS 360, LW 305, MG 306, MG 333, MK 440, MK 442, MK 444, PL 314/TH 312, SA 224, SA 360/PT 360, WR 325, BL 109, BL 315, SA 364, CS 496. With M&O chair approval: AC 201, IB 282, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240.
### Management Consulting Minor (poid 5966) — not for Management majors
MG 201, MG 306, MG 402, MG 407; first elective: one of MG 304, MG 305, MG 333, MG 410, MG 412, MG 414, MG 420, MG 453; second elective: one from a broad list (AC 202, AC 311, AC 402, AC 421, CM 310, CM 314, CM 346, EC 302, EC 405, EC 420, EC 460, EG 441, FI 350, FI 440, FI 441, IB 282, IB/MG 300, IS 353, IS 355, IS 358, IS 453, IS 460, MG electives above, MK 346, MK 441, MK 444, MK 447, MK 451, MK 452, OM 305, OM 334, OM 335, OM 406, PY 201, PY 203, PY 253, PY 262, PY 310, PY 351, SC 201, SC 202, WR 220, WR 301, WR 305, WR 320, WR 325, WR 326).
### Supply Chain Management Minor (poid 5964)
18 credits: EC 220 or ST 210; IS 251 or CS 151 or DS 303; OM 260; two of OM 305, OM 334, OM 335, OM 406; plus one of EC 304, EC 360, EC 405, EC 425, EC 446, IB 415, IS 301, IS 358, IS 360, IS 460, LW 406, LW 410, LW 411, MA 481, MA 485, MG 333, MK 415, MK 441.

### Business Journalism Minor (poid 5982)
EC 102, EC 103, CM 201, CM 304; one journalism elective (CM 203, CM 301, CM 318, CM 385, CM 391, WR 305); one business elective (AC 201, AC 409, AC 412, FI 210, LW 305, EC 304, EC 305, EC 347, EC 348, EC 360, EC 380, EC 405, EC 430, EC 440, EC 450, EC 475, EC 480, EC 490, IB 282 or BH 282, MG 222, MK 240).
### Arts Management Minor (poid 6205)
AC 201, AH 201, CM 307 or MK 240, MG 201, plus two 300/400-level AH, PT, SA, MU, or DR courses in the same discipline (an approved internship can count for one). BH 200/201/240 may replace AC 201/MG 201/MK 240.

## CLOSED legacy programs (closed to new students — current students may still be on them)
### Business Administration, Finance Concentration, BBA (poid 5959) — CLOSED
Required: FI 322, FI 340, FI 350, FI 440, FI 441, LW 305. FOUR finance electives from FI 330, FI 380, FI 426, FI 430, FI 431, FI 433, FI 434, FI 499; up to two may come from EC 301, EC 302, EC 420, EC 430, AC 301, AC 302, AC 303. FI 121 does not count.
### Business Administration, Information Systems Concentration, BBA (poid 5960) — CLOSED
Required: AC 202, IS 352, IS 353, IS 355, IS 358, IS 453, IS 458, LW 305. Free-elective options: IS 356, IS 360, IS 452, IS 459, IS 460, IS 499, OM 334, OM 335. CS 151 may substitute for IS 352 with chair approval.
### Business Administration, Management Concentration, BBA (poid 5961) — CLOSED
Foundation for this concentration listed as AC 201, EC 102, EC 103, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260. Required: IB 282, LW 305, MG 304, MG 305, MG 306, MG 402, MG 407, plus two of MG 333, MG 410, MG 412, MG 414, MG 419, MG 453. MG 402 and MG 407 may be taken fall or spring if prerequisites are met.
### Business Administration, Marketing Concentration, BBA (poid 5963) — CLOSED
Required: AC 202, LW 305, MK 346, MK 440, MK 441, MK 442. THREE marketing electives from MK 349, MK 415, MK 444, MK 447, MK 448, MK 449, MK 450, MK 451, MK 452, MK 453, MK 499; optionally one of CM 355, CM 358, PY 201, PY 222, SC 342, ST 465 may count as a marketing elective.
