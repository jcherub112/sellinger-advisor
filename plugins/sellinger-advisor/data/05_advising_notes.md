# Sellinger Advising Notes — sequencing tips (LOWEST authority after career file)
Rules from the audit, program requirements, course master, and policies ALWAYS override this file. These are faculty rules of thumb, rebuilt on the 2026–27 catalog (2026-10-06).

## Gateway chains (verified against 2026–27 prerequisites)
- Foundation finance: AC 201 + EC 102 (+ EC 220/ST 210 before or same term) → FI 230 → everything in FI. FI 322 gates FI 431, FI 433, FI 440, FI 441.
- Finance BS critical path: FI 230 (yr 1–2) → FI 322 → FI 440 (FALL only) and FI 441 (SPRING only, seniors). Missing FI 440 in senior fall is the classic late-graduation trap.
- FI 330 Junior Analyst Class: sophomore Finance majors only, Spring only, needs AC 201 + FI 230 in the first year.
- Statistics: EC 220 now requires IS 251 → take IS 251 before or alongside the statistics plan. ST 210 needs MA 109/ALEKS/HS calculus instead.
- OM 260 needs MA 151/251, EC 102, EC 220 or ST 210 (IS 251 may be concurrent).
- Accounting: AC 201 → AC 202 → (AC 311). AC 201 → AC 301 (FALL) → AC 302 (SPRING), AC 303 (FALL), AC 401/402/409 (SPRING). Fall start of AC 301 is essential.
- ISDA: IS 251 → IS 352, IS 353, IS 355, IS 420 (FALL), IS 453/454; IS 358 needs EC 220/ST 210 + MA 151/251 (+ IS 353 concurrent). IS 458 (SPRING) needs IS 352, IS 353, IS 358 → it is a final-spring course.
- Marketing: MK 240 (sophomore+) → MK 346; most MK 4xx need MK 240 + 60 credits; MK 441 and MK 442 are senior-restricted.
- Management: MG 201 (sophomore+) → MG 304/305/306 (juniors+) → MG 402/MG 407 (seniors).
- FRMI: FRI 300 + FRI 305 (both FALL) gate all other FRI courses (all FALL). Plan FRI 300/305 by junior fall.
- Real Estate: REE 325 and REE 335 are FALL; REE 445 and REE 455 are SPRING; REE 465 needs REE 345, 355, 445, 455.
- Supply Chain: OM 305 FALL, OM 334 SPRING, OM 335 FALL (juniors+), OM 406 either term (juniors+).
- LW 305 requires 60 credits (junior standing).

## Planning habits
1. First year: EC 102, EC 103, MA 151/251, AC 201 (if the major's sample plan has it), WR 100, Core.
2. Finish the six-course foundation by the end of sophomore year when possible (some majors' sample plans push FI 230/OM 260 later — follow the major's plan).
3. Put Fall-only and Spring-only major courses on the plan FIRST, then fill with Core and electives.
4. Keep the final year lighter with some flexibility — don't leave a single-term, senior-only course with no backup term.
5. Default 5 courses per semester. A sixth course is for a real bottleneck, not a default (see policy for eligibility).
6. Double majors: shared Business Foundation counts for both; anything else needs the audit or worksheet to show it. IB + Sustainability Management: only ONE course may double count beyond shared foundation/required courses.
7. Study abroad: look for a term with no Fall/Spring-only major requirements (commonly sophomore spring or junior fall, but check the student's specific chain). Core courses travel best. All courses abroad need pre-approval.
8. Summer: Loyola offers many foundation courses in summer (AC 201, AC 202, FI 230, MG 201, IS 358, EC 220, MA 151). Courses at another school need prior written AASC approval and a C or better.

## Common mistakes to catch
- Planning a course in a term it isn't offered (validator catches this).
- Planning a senior- or junior-restricted course too early.
- Treating "Recommended Prerequisite" as optional when the student is weak in that area — mention it.
- Forgetting Diversity-Justice (2 courses) and the Language core.
- Assuming a course double counts when the audit doesn't show it.
