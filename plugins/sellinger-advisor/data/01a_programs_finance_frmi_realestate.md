# Sellinger Program Requirements — Finance Frmi Realestate — 2026–2027 Undergraduate Catalogue
Source: catalogue.loyola.edu (catoid=43), program pages pulled 2026-10-06. poid = catalog program ID (URL: https://catalogue.loyola.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=43&poid=XXXX).
AUTHORITY: authoritative for 2026–27 program requirements. A student's DegreeWorks audit governs THEIR catalog year — if the audit's catalog year is earlier and differs from this file, follow the audit and flag the difference.
Every BBA and BS (Finance, FRMI, Real Estate) major ALSO completes the six-course Business Foundation (AC 201, FI 230, IS 251, MG 201, MK 240, OM 260), EC 102, EC 103, MA 151 or MA 251, EC 220 (or ST 210, except Business Economics), the Loyola Core, and 2 Diversity-Justice courses — see 03_policies_core_and_rules.md.
Sample plans ("typical program") are ONE possible path, not requirements.

QUICK ANSWERS (Finance BS, 2026–27):
- Required: EC 102, EC 103, FI 322, FI 350, FI 430, FI 440, FI 441, LW 305 (+ six-course Business Foundation, MA 151/251, EC 220 or ST 210).
- Finance electives (choose 4): FI 210; FI 225 or REE 225; FI 300 or FRI 300; FI 305 or FRI 305; FI 315; FI 325 or REE 325; FI 330; FI 340; FI 380; FI 426; FI 431; FI 433; FI 434; FI 499; FRI 350; FRI 410; FRI 450; REE 445. Up to 2 of the 4 may instead be AC 301, AC 302, AC 311, AC 412, EC 420, EC 425, EC 430, FR 310, IS 420, IS 460, MG 433, MK 440, MK 441, OM 335, or any MA/ST 300–400 level course.
- REAL ESTATE courses that count as Finance BS electives: FI 225 / REE 225 (Real Estate), FI 325 / REE 325 (Real Estate Finance and Investment), REE 445 (Real Estate Modeling and Valuation). No other REE course counts toward the Finance BS. (There are no "RE 201/RE 301" courses.)
- RISK courses that count as Finance BS electives: FI/FRI 300, FI/FRI 305, FRI 350, FRI 410, FRI 450.

## Finance, BS (poid 5915)  ← the active Finance major (the BBA Finance concentration is closed to new students)
Major requirements: EC 102, EC 103, FI 322, FI 350, FI 430, FI 440, FI 441, LW 305.
FOUR finance electives from: FI 210; FI 225 or REE 225; FI 300 or FRI 300; FI 305 or FRI 305; FI 315; FI 325 or REE 325; FI 330; FI 340; FI 380; FI 426; FI 431; FI 433; FI 434; FI 499; FRI 350; FRI 410; FRI 450; REE 445.
Up to TWO of the four electives may instead come from: AC 301, AC 302, AC 311, AC 412, EC 420, EC 425, EC 430, FR 310, IS 420, IS 460, MG 433, MK 440, MK 441, OM 335, any MA 300/400-level, any ST 300/400-level.
Notes: FI 330 counts as a finance elective, ONLY for sophomore finance majors, and only if AC 201 and FI 230 were taken in the first year. ST 210 may substitute for EC 220. ELMBA accelerated: EL 703 in lieu of FI 426.
Typical plan: Y1 F: AC 201, EC 102, EN 101, MA 151/251, WR 100 | Y1 S: EC 103, EC 220, FI 230, HS 100, PL 201 | Y2 F: FI 322, FI 330 or EN/HS 200-level, IS 251, TH 201, Language | Y2 S: FI 350, LW 305, MK 240, OM 260, PL/TH 200-level | Y3 F: FI 430, Finance Elective or EN/HS 200-level, Fine Arts, Nat Sci, Elective | Y3 S: MG 201, Finance Elective, Ethics, 2 Electives | Y4 F: FI 440 (Fall only), Finance Elective, 3 Electives | Y4 S: FI 441 (Spring only, seniors), Finance Elective, 3 Electives.
Sequencing: FI 322 gates FI 431, FI 433, FI 440, FI 441. FI 440 is Fall only and FI 441 is Spring only — a student who misses FI 440 in senior fall cannot graduate on time without an exception.

## Financial Risk Management and Insurance, BS (poid 6197)
Required: EC 102, EC 103, EC 220 (ST 210 may substitute), FI 300 or FRI 300, FI 305 or FRI 305, FI 322, FI 431, FRI 450, LW 305, MA 151 or MA 251, ST 310.
TWO FRMI electives from: FRI 350, FRI 410, FRI 426, FRI 499, IS 358, IS 370, IS 420, MA 485, ST 461, ST 465, ST 473, ST 475.
TWO finance electives from: FI 340, FI 350, FI 380, FI 430, FI 433, FI 434, FI 440, FI 441.
Sequencing: nearly every FRI course is FALL ONLY in the 2026–27 catalog (FRI 300, 305, 350, 410, 426, 450, 499). FRI 300 and FRI 305 gate the rest. Missing them in a junior fall pushes the remaining FRI courses into senior fall — flag any plan that doesn't take FRI 300/305 by junior fall. [Sample plan not extracted; see catalog.]

## Construction Management and Real Estate Development, BS (poid 6208 Construction Engineering conc.; poid 6209 Real Estate Development conc.)
Interdisciplinary with Engineering; heavy required load — always flag four-year feasibility for the advisor.
6209 (Real Estate Development) required: AC 201, CMR 201, CMR 202, CMR 301, CMR 310, CMR 401, EC 102, EC 103, FI 230, FI 325 or REE 325, MA 251, MG 201, REE 365, REE 445, SN 104, WR 325 or WR 326; plus three of CMR 311, CMR 312, CMR 313, IS 352, MG 304, MG 305, MG 306, MK 240, LW 406, REE 435.
6208 (Construction Engineering) required: AC 201, CMR 201, CMR 202, CMR 301, CMR 310, CMR 401, EC 102, EC 103, EG 301, EG 305, EG 307, EG 309, EG 311, EG 313, EG 351 + EG 051, MA 251, MG 201, PH 201 + PH 291, SN 104, WR 325 or WR 326.
Typical 6209 plan: Y1 F: EC 102, MA 251, WR 100, Nat Sci, Elective | Y1 S: EC 103, SN 104, Math/NatSci, 2 Electives | Y2 F: AC 201, CMR 201, HS 100, Fine Arts | Y2 S: CMR 202, EN 101, FI 230, MG 201 | Y3 F: FI/REE 325, PL 201, TH 201, WR 325/326, Elective | Y3 S: CMR 301, REE 445, Major Elective, EN/HS 200-level | Y4 F: CMR 310, REE 365, Ethics, PL/TH 200-level, Major Elective | Y4 S: CMR 401, Major Elective, 2 Electives.
