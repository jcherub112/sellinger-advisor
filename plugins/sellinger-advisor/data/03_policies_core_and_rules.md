# Loyola / Sellinger Academic Policies — authoritative extract
Source: Loyola University Maryland 2026–2027 Undergraduate Academic Catalogue (catalogue.loyola.edu, catoid=43), "Curriculum and Policies" and "Sellinger School" pages. Pulled 2026-10-06.
Authority: AUTHORITATIVE for policy (6th course, residency, standing, core, transfer). Students on an older catalog year may have different rules — flag if the audit shows a different catalog year.

## Sellinger Business Foundation (2026–27 catalog)
BBA and BS (Finance, Financial Risk Management & Insurance, Real Estate) majors complete SIX business foundation courses:
- AC 201 Financial Accounting
- FI 230 Financial Management
- IS 251 Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, and Information Systems
- MG 201 Management
- MK 240 Marketing
- OM 260 Supply Chain and Operations Management

Sellinger Scholars take honors equivalents: BH 200 (Fin Acct), BH 201 (Mgmt), BH 230 (Fin Mgmt), BH 240 (Mktg), BH 251 (Data Analytics/IS), BH 260 (Supply Chain/Ops), BH 305 Legal Environment (or BH 406/LW 406 Commercial Law for Accounting majors only), BH 403 Sellinger Scholar Consulting Practicum. Plus BH 196 (first year) and BH 199 (senior seminar) one-credit courses.

All Sellinger majors also complete: EC 102, EC 103, and MA 151 or MA 251.
Upper-division standing = 60 credits completed, including MA 151 or MA 251 (or equivalent).
BBA candidates (except Business Economics) may take ST 210 in place of EC 220. EC 220 and ST 210 both count toward the third Math/Natural Science Loyola Core requirement.
Diversity-Justice: two designated Diversity-Justice courses required, anywhere in the curriculum.

LEGACY NUMBERING: FI 320 / BH 320 (Financial Management) is the older number for the foundation finance course; FI 230 / BH 230 is current. Prerequisite lists still accept "FI 230 or BH 230 or FI 320 or BH 320". A student with FI 320 on their audit has satisfied the foundation finance course. Older catalogs listed an 8-course foundation that included EC 102/EC 103/FI 320 — trust the student's audit for their own catalog year.

## Loyola Core (all students; Honors Program uses an alternative core)
- Composition: WR 100 Effective Writing
- Ethics: one course PL 300–319 or TH 300–319
- Fine Arts: one of AH 109, AH 110, AH 111, MU 201–204, PT 270, SA 224, SA 227/EG 227, DR 250, DR 251, DR 252
- History: HS 100 Encountering the Past
- Literature: EN 101 The Art of Reading
- One additional 200-level EN or 200-level HS course
- Language: one course at the 104 or 200 level in a modern language (Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Spanish), or Greek/Latin at 104 or 300 level; normally completed at Loyola; required of all students
- Mathematics: one MA/ST course (excluding MA 103, MA 104)
- Natural Science: one natural science course (incl. ENV 114)
- Math/Natural Science: one additional course in CS, EC 220, EG 101/EG 103, MA/ST (not MA 103/104), or a natural science
- Philosophy: PL 201 Foundations of Philosophy
- Theology: TH 201 Introduction to Christian Theology
- One additional 200-level Philosophical Perspectives course OR one course from TH 202–299 — must be in the department OTHER than the one used for Ethics
- Social Sciences: two survey courses from economics, political science, psychology, or sociology (usually 100-level; 200-level for PY and select SC). ED 100, ED 205, ENV 116 count for one.
- Diversity-Justice: two designated courses
Note for Sellinger students: EC 102 and EC 103 are economics survey courses (the audit shows how they are applied).

## Degree totals and electives
Minimum 120 credits and 38 courses. Free electives = whatever remains after core + major; number depends on the major.

## Sixth Course (OVERLOAD) POLICY — use exactly
- During official registration, students may register for only FIVE courses (one-/two-credit courses don't count toward the five).
- A sixth course requires electronic permission from the academic advisor before registering (or a Change of Registration Form with the sixth-course box checked, signed by the advisor, submitted to the Academic Advising and Support Center).
- First-year students may request a sixth course for their SECOND semester only if they earned a QPA of 3.000 or higher in their first semester (five 3-, 4-, or 5-credit courses).
- Upperclassmen must be in good academic standing (cumulative QPA 2.000 or above) to request a sixth course.
- Approval is subject to completing all prerequisites and course availability.
- Hard caps: no more than six 3- or 4-credit courses in fall or spring; no more than one course in the winter away period; no more than five total summer courses (no more than two external summer away courses). More than the equivalent of two summer courses in the same summer session is not recommended.
TOOL RULE: report "Eligible to REQUEST a sixth course" or "Not eligible to request a sixth course," with the reason. Never say "approved." Approval belongs to the advisor.

## Good academic standing
First-year and transfer students: cumulative QPA at least 1.800 at the end of the first and second semesters. From the third semester onward: at least 2.000.

## Residency
At least 60 credits at Loyola. Of the last 60 credits, 45 must be at Loyola. 15 of the last 21 credits, and at least half the courses in the major and the minor, must be at Loyola. Placement-test credit doesn't count toward residency. Loyola's own study abroad programs/affiliations and BSEP count toward residency; external study abroad programs do not count toward residency or the Loyola QPA.

## Courses at other colleges / transfer
All transfer courses need prior written permission from the Academic Advising and Support Center. Grade of C (2.000) or higher required (except BSEP and approved international programs); only credits transfer, not grades.

## Repeating a course
Repeats of required core or major courses must be the same course (failed core-area courses may be repeated by another course in that area). Both grades count in the cumulative QPA unless grade forgiveness is used (maximum two D/D+/U/F grades forgiven; Repeat/Replacement Form via AASC). Repeats cannot be registered electronically. Financial aid pays for only one repeat of a previously passed course.

## Change of major / minor
Change of major requires advisor and both department chairs' consent plus the Request for Change of Major/Minor Form (AASC). Loyola does not guarantee four-year completion after a major change. Minors: Change of Minor Form signed by the advisor.

## Study abroad
Details through the Office of International Programs (loyola.edu/department/international-programs). Courses taken abroad must be pre-approved. Study-abroad equivalencies are NOT in this knowledge base yet — tell students to get course pre-approval from International Programs and their advisor.
